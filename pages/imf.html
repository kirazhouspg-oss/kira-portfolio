Variation Amounts of Decade Loans
COVID-19 was one of the worst global events of the last decade, with a major impact on everyone's life, so we visualized the changes in global lending data over the last decade.








PROCESS
My group and I went through the database provided by the International Monetary Fund and thought about related topics. Visit the official IMF website for more information. We focused on the changes in global lending patterns during COVID-19, delving into the stories and reasons for them to highlight the relationship with changes in total lending during COVID-19.






PROCESS
In the beginning, we were going to make a poster that included only the last three years of data, we listed all the countries we are lending to in 2019-2021, and categorized them by Europe, Americas, Asia, Oceania, and Africa, and then calculated their three-year lending totals in Google Sheets.







OUTCOME
Poster Display
