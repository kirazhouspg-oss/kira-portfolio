Brand Upgrade

One Collection is a brand upgrade for Select Property’s premium residential portfolio, scheduled for official launch in 2027. This page presents only a selected portion of the brand proposal.

One Collection 是 Select Property 旗下高端住宅系列的品牌升级项目，预计于 2027 年正式发布，本页面仅展示部分品牌提案内容。

Through One Collection and other branding projects, I have developed and led a complete brand design process spanning research and insight, brand positioning, visual strategy, identity systems, application guidelines, and multi-touchpoint implementation. My experience covers luxury real estate, industrial, hospitality, and consumer brands, as well as building my own pet brand, KaWa Room, from the ground up, demonstrating strong capabilities in brand strategy, systematic design, and long-term brand asset management.

通过 One Collection 及其他品牌项目，我建立并推进了从研究洞察、品牌定位、视觉策略，到识别系统、应用规范及跨触点落地的完整品牌设计流程。相关经验覆盖高端地产、工业、餐饮及消费品牌，并延伸至自有品牌 KaWa Room 的从 0 到 1 建设，体现了我在品牌策略、系统化设计与品牌资产管理方面的综合能力。











 
Sales & Digital Materials

Led visual design for SPG’s marketing campaigns and sales enablement materials across Asian markets, including Mainland China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Singapore, Malaysia, Japan, and South Korea.

主导SPG项目在亚洲市场的营销广告与销售支持内容的视觉设计，覆盖中国大陆、香港、台湾、新加坡、马来西亚及日韩市场。

Oversaw multi-channel marketing assets including property brochures, investment guides, sales pitch decks, EDMs, WeChat content, Meta advertising, and social media. Adapted visual strategies to local audience profiles, media channels, and commercial objectives, ensuring brand consistency while improving market relevance and sales support across regions.

统筹楼书、投资指南、销售 Pitch Deck、EDM、公众号、Meta广告及社交媒体等多渠道营销资产，并结合不同市场的受众特征、传播渠道与商业目标进行本地化视觉策略调整，确保品牌在各区域保持一致的同时具备更强的市场适配性与销售转化支持。


UI & UX

Designed the Select Property WeChat Mini Program, integrating brand identity, property information, and sales content into one digital platform. Structured the user journey and information hierarchy to help users explore projects, access key details, and make enquiries efficiently.

为 Select Property 设计微信小程序，将品牌视觉、楼盘信息与销售内容整合至统一的数字平台。围绕用户浏览路径、信息架构与页面层级进行整体规划，使用户能够更高效地了解项目、获取关键信息并完成进一步咨询。
