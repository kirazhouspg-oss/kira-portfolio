KaWa Room is a lifestyle brand designed around pet nutrition and everyday companionship, using a playful and approachable visual and content language to make mindful pet feeding feel more natural, engaging, and warm.

KaWa Room 是为宠物饮食与日常陪伴打造的生活方式品牌，通过轻松、有趣的视觉与内容语言，让科学宠物喂养变得更自然、更有温度。



IP

KaWa Room is built around its “Chef Dog” IP, creating a distinctive and emotionally engaging brand identity that extends across packaging, social media, collaborations, and merchandise.

以“主厨狗狗”作为核心品牌 IP，将宠物零食从单纯的产品消费延伸为更具情感连接与记忆点的品牌体验。通过统一的角色形象、内容表达与品牌应用，建立可识别的 IP 资产，并为社交传播、产品包装及周边设计提供长期价值。


WeChat Mini Program

Starting from the brand’s visual identity system, I independently designed the KaWa Room WeChat Mini Program interface (UI) and planned the overall user experience (UX), extending the brand language across key digital touchpoints. By structuring the information architecture, page hierarchy, purchase journey, and content flow, I maintained brand consistency while improving browsing efficiency and conversion experience.

从品牌视觉体系出发，我独立完成 KaWa Room 微信小程序的界面设计 ( UI ) 与用户体验规划 ( UX ) ，将品牌语言延展至完整数字触点。通过信息架构、页面层级、购买路径与内容节奏的梳理，在保持品牌一致性的同时提升浏览效率与转化体验。


Digital Brand Experience

Built a cohesive digital visual system across e-commerce and social media, unifying product presentation, marketing campaigns, and brand communications. Integrated AIGC into the visual production workflow to enhance product imagery and content creation, improving both quality and iteration speed while reducing traditional photography and production costs.

建立覆盖电商、社交媒体的品牌数字视觉体系，统一产品展示、营销广告与内容传播的视觉语言。结合 AIGC 优化产品图与视觉素材生产流程，在提升内容质感与迭代效率的同时，降低传统拍摄与制作成本。
